# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-01**

## Overview

This kanji study app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. No account registration is required or possible.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Study progress (kanji and vocabulary learned/unlearned status)
- Quiz session history
- App settings (theme, audio volume)
- JLPT practice test progress

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, shared with any third party, or accessible to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses no third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or SDKs that collect data.

## Internet Access

This app does not require or use an internet connection.

## Data Deletion

You can reset all progress and settings from within the app (Settings → Reset). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
